## The three

### 1. Kirloskar Ferrous Industries (NSE: KIRLFER)

- **Business:** pig iron and iron/steel castings — a foundry business. Buys iron ore and
  coke, runs blast furnaces and induction furnaces, sells pig iron and machined castings
  mostly into the auto, tractor and engine component supply chain (including group
  companies). One product family, one process, no segments to untangle.
- **Market cap:** ~₹6,900–7,600 cr through 2026 ([Screener.in](https://www.screener.in/company/KIRLFER/), [ValueResearch](https://www.valueresearchonline.com/stocks/42958/kirloskar-ferrous-industries-ltd/)). Comfortably inside the band.
- **Coverage:** effectively none — Trendlyne shows 0 recent broker reports with a target
  price, and Simply Wall St flags analyst coverage as "Low" ([mlq.ai](https://mlq.ai/stocks/KIRLFER.NS/price-target/), [Trendlyne](https://trendlyne.com/research-reports/post/KIRLFER/2056/kirloskar-ferrous-industries-ltd/)).
- **History:** demerged from Kirloskar Ferrous into its current listed form in 2009, with
  the group's foundry business going back decades before that — well past ten years of
  annual reports.
- **Cyclicality gives real data:** pig iron/casting margins move with steel prices and the
  auto cycle, so ten years of history should show visible growth swings rather than a flat
  line manufactured for the write-up.

### 2. Balaji Amines (NSE: BALAMINES)

- **Business:** aliphatic amines (methylamines, ethylamines) and their derivatives, sold as
  chemical intermediates into pharma, agrochemical and other downstream manufacturers.
  Slightly more technical than a foundry but still one integrated chemistry platform, not a
  conglomerate of segments.
- **Market cap:** ~₹7,200–8,300 cr through 2026 ([ICICI Direct](https://www.icicidirect.com/stocks/balaji-amines-ltd-share-price)). Inside the band.
- **Coverage:** thin — Trendlyne and other consensus trackers show 1–3 analysts actively
  rating it depending on the snapshot date, well below what a company this size would
  normally attract.
- **History:** listed since the 1990s, well past ten years of filings.

### 3. Time Technoplast (NSE: TIMETECHNO)

- **Business:** industrial polymer packaging (drums, IBCs, composite cylinders) plus smaller
  lifestyle-products and auto-components lines. The core packaging business is easy to
  explain; the side segments are the reason this sits third rather than first — they add
  real complexity to "explain the business end to end."
- **Market cap:** ~₹9,200–9,300 cr, down about 20% over the past year ([stockanalysis.com](https://stockanalysis.com/quote/nse/TIMETECHNO/)). Inside the band.
- **Coverage:** described as "Low" in third-party screens; no bulge-bracket names show up in
  the search.
- **History:** listed since 1992, well past ten years of filings.
